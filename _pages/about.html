I am a Master of Science in Computer Science (MSCS) student at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, working with Prof. Minjia Zhang at the SSAIL Lab. My research focuses on large-scale vector databases on GPUs, efficient data management systems, and high-performance computing for AI applications.
